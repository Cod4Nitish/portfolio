What I do

What does an AI engineer actually build?

In plain words, here are the three things I do — each with a real project you can look at.

01

AI agents that finish tasks

Software that can plan a job, use tools, and carry it through — not just chat. It saves a team the repetitive follow-up work.

Example: MRStay AI qualifies real-estate leads and follows up with customers. ForgeMind triages GitHub issues and coordinates Jira and Slack.

See agent projects →
02

AI that answers from your documents

You give it your own files, rules or notes. It answers only from them and shows where the answer came from, so you can trust it.

Example: Bhumi Help answers Bhopal building-rule questions in Hindi or English with the rule number and the exact PDF page.

See document-AI projects →
03

The full product around the AI

The website, the backend API and the database that turn a smart idea into something people can open and use — fast and easy to understand.

Example: CallbackCV checks a résumé for Indian freshers and shows clear, role-aware feedback.
